Roughly 20 additional badge *candidates* emerge from a child's actual learning history. Avoid generic grind, leaderboard pressure, or judging a child's worth based on badge scarcity.

## Two orthogonal axes
1. Moment significance / scene hierarchy (visual composition and entrance animation, NOT a person's ability rank):
   - POCKET · **주머니 발견** (20): tiny camp pin / stitched ribbon; familiar, warm, lightly funny.
   - FIELD · **현장 표창** (20): expedition field patch / compass stripe; a clearly captured useful moment.
   - EXPEDITION · **원정 공적** (14): compass wreath / contour map; a memorable changed approach or completed journey.
   - SECRET · **비밀 훈장** (6): concealed constellation / aurora margin; special story reveal. Secrets must not depend on unsafe, excessive or compulsory performance.
2. A badge's repeat-acquisition star progression is strictly SEPARATE: first verified award unlocks at 0 stars, every *distinct approved reaward* adds one, 5 triggers tier progression. Moment significance is not GREEN/BLUE/RED/GOLD/PLATINUM and doesn't grant gems/EXP/power.

## UX: single-screen switching (no long scroll)
One Child collection has 3 primary segments: **도감 60 / 나의 발견 / 획득 기록**. Within preset collection, filters are short, not a competition board: **전체 · 작은 순간 · 현장 · 원정 · 비밀**. Existing active/approved but unearned badge art appears only as a soft, low-color silhouette; an unapproved base visual is not displayed as if it were an approved silhouette. Discovered history candidates live in *나의 발견*, initially in a mist/treasure-envelope slot rather than pre-crediting the child. Candidate details show what genuine history evidence was connected and that review is pending, with no secret user data shown to other children or unauthorized family members.

Entry animation stages: ① moment-specific clue (blanket/sprout/map) → ② fictional exploration crew insignia assembles with pastel thread → ③ child's own profile avatar appears as a distinct scoped layer → ④ acquired badge and verified current within-tier stars are displayed → ⑤ soft radial/conic gradient border dissipates. Locked entries skip child overlay and earned stars. Reduced-motion alternative displays a static reveal.

Family Praise Gift badges are a distinct, only separately-approved giftable catalogue: not a shortcut to this 60+20 learning achievement criteria, and not a parent's personal locked atlas. A family member may send 1–5 gems per gift only with an explicit verified permission.

## Dynamic evidence and review
The 20 templates use meaningful pairs of distinct history evidence kinds, e.g. corrected+revisited error, assisted+unassisted, spaced review+independent recall, requested help+continued alone, strategy change+verified result, replan+completion, question+tested evidence. They are exploratory semantic hypotheses, **not already-approved criteria**. Actual Learning Engine evidence kind mappings, trusted source verification and review decision authority remain OPEN. The pure `badge-story-discovery.js` requires an injected trusted verifier and matched family/child receipt scope, rejects duplicate and foreign receipts, and only returns `REVIEW_REQUIRED_NOT_AWARD` candidates. It does NOT access actual app history or write an Award Ledger.
