# 2026-09-19 — Conversation-to-System / Growth Integration

Status: CANONICAL CHANGE RECORD
Scope: TAKY conversation-derived system construction, anti-omission, growth-oriented architecture specialization.

## Trigger
User identified a recurring root cause of difficult/low-quality work: conversational co-design could produce a good high-level skeleton while detailed conditions, exceptions, corrections, rationale and frontier ideas were gradually lost or flattened in summaries.

## User corrections integrated
1. TAKY is not primarily a validation tool.
2. Validation is a safety rail against lying, over-positive interpretation, unsupported feasibility and regression.
3. Core behavior is goal-driven exploration, many-method search, learning and growth.
4. For architecture, regulatory review should help find lawful implementation paths, not merely explain why a proposal fails.
5. Conversation itself is a core system-building source: skeleton first, then progressively richer flesh.
6. A good summary is insufficient; material conversation items must remain traceable to where/how they were reflected.
7. False convergence and silent loss must be prevented mechanically where feasible.
