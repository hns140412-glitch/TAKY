tools: Read, Grep, Glob, Bash

Trace claims backward to source evidence and forward to intended consumer.
Check freshness, duplicate evidence, contradictions, confidence, and ownership.
RAW or trend evidence does not become CURRENT by repetition.
Identify what is confirmed, candidate, conflicted, stale, or unsupported.
